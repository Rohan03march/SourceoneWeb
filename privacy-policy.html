1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how SourceOne Management Services ("SourceOne", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, protects, and discloses personal information when candidates, employees, or authorized personnel access and use the SourceOne Onboarding mobile application and associated web services ("App").

Important Notice: By downloading, installing, or logging into the SourceOne Onboarding App, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws, including the Indian Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

2. Information We Collect

To facilitate seamless employment onboarding, identity verification, background check, and payroll processing, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Identification Information

  • Full Legal Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Father's Name, Mother's Name, Spouse Name.
  • Contact Details: Mobile Phone Number, Email Address, Present Residential Address, Permanent Address.
  • Emergency Contacts: Name, Relationship, and Phone Number of designated emergency contacts.
  • Blood Group & Marital Status.

B. Government Identifiers & Statutory Documents

  • Aadhaar Card: Card number, virtual ID, and digital scan/image for identity verification.
  • Permanent Account Number (PAN): PAN number and document scan for tax compliance.
  • Voter ID / Driving License / Passport: Identity and address proof documents where applicable.
  • Bank Account Details: Bank Name, Account Number, Account Holder Name, and IFSC Code (for salary deposit and payroll execution).
  • PF / ESIC Details: Universal Account Number (UAN), ESIC IP Number, and prior employment details.

C. Employment & Educational Background

  • Resume/CV, educational qualification certificates, work experience certificates, prior salary slips, and resignation/relieving letters.

3. Camera Usage, Facial Data & Photo Verification

The SourceOne Onboarding App requests access to your device's Camera and Photo Gallery for specific identity verification functions.

  • Profile & ID Photo Capture: You are required to capture a live photo (selfie) or upload a photo to generate your official employee badge and verify your physical identity.
  • Document Scanning: The camera is used to capture clean images of mandatory onboarding documents (e.g., Aadhaar card, PAN card, bank passbook, educational certificates).
  • Liveness & Identity Check: Live facial images captured during onboarding are used solely for candidate identity verification to prevent impersonation and fraud.

Facial Data Policy: Facial images and captured photos are used strictly for identity verification and official employee ID generation. We do not sell, rent, or trade your facial data or photo records to third parties or advertising networks.

4. App Permissions Required & Purpose

When using the SourceOne Onboarding mobile application, the app may prompt you to grant the following runtime permissions:

CAMERA

Required to take live profile photos/selfies and scan physical identity documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Bank Passbook).

STORAGE / MEDIA / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Required to select and upload document files (PDFs, images) stored on your mobile device (e.g., resume, marksheet certificates).

LOCATION (FINE/COARSE)

Used where required to verify address location or job deployment site during physical onboarding verification.

NOTIFICATIONS

Used to deliver onboarding progress alerts, document verification status updates, and operational notices.

5. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate employment and operational purposes:

  • Candidate Onboarding: Verifying identity, processing employment applications, and conducting background checks.
  • Statutory Compliance: Enrolling employees into Employee Provident Fund (EPF), Employee State Insurance (ESIC), Labour Welfare Fund, and Income Tax deductions.
  • Payroll Processing: Disbursing salaries, allowances, and managing direct bank transfers.
  • Operational Management: Creating employee records, managing attendance, issuing official ID badges, and allocating work assignments.
  • Communication: Sending SMS, email, or in-app updates regarding onboarding status, document corrections, or company announcements.

6. Data Sharing & Statutory Disclosures

We respect your privacy and handle your data with strict confidentiality. We share your information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Statutory & Government Bodies: As mandated by law, data is shared with government entities such as the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), ESIC, Income Tax Department, and relevant labor welfare boards.
  • Client Deployment Organizations: For manpower and security deployment, basic candidate profile details (Name, Photo, Qualification, Verification status) may be shared with the client site where you are assigned to work.
  • Service Providers & Cloud Infrastructure: Secure cloud servers, SMS/email gateway providers, and identity verification partners who operate under binding confidentiality contracts.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by court order, law enforcement investigation, or applicable legal process.

No Commercial Sale: SourceOne does NOT sell, market, lease, or monetize your personal data to commercial advertisers or third-party telemarketers under any circumstances.

7. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the mobile application and our servers is encrypted using standard Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (SSL/TLS) protocols.
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive data, stored documents, and database entries are encrypted using industry-standard encryption algorithms.
  • Access Control: Strict role-based access controls (RBAC) ensure that only authorized HR and compliance personnel can view candidate onboarding documents.

8. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion

We retain candidate and employee personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the onboarding process, employment relationship, and legal statutory obligations under applicable labor laws.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

If you wish to request the deletion of your account or personal data collected through the SourceOne Onboarding App, you can visit our dedicated Account Deletion Request Page or follow these steps:

  • Send an email to info@sourceone.in with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - SourceOne Onboarding App".
  • Include your Full Name, Registered Phone Number / Email Address, and Employee/Candidate ID.
  • Our Data Compliance team will review and process your request within 7 to 15 business days.
Go to Dedicated Account Deletion Page

Note: Statutory records (e.g., PF/ESI filings, tax records, legal employment logs) may be retained beyond account deletion as required by Indian labor and tax statutes.

9. Your Rights & Choices

As a user of the SourceOne Onboarding App, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & View: Review your profile details and uploaded documents within the App.
  • Correction & Updating: Request corrections to inaccurate or outdated personal details.
  • Permission Revocation: Revoke app permissions (Camera, Storage, Location) anytime through your mobile device system settings.
  • Consent Withdrawal: Withdraw consent for data processing, subject to statutory employment conditions.

10. Children's Privacy

The SourceOne Onboarding App is intended strictly for employment candidate onboarding and is restricted to individuals aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from minors under the age of 18.
